rocket_launch Getting Started

play_circle

How do I create my first journal entry?

After signing up, tap the "+" button on the home screen. You'll be taken to the rich text editor powered by Quill. Write your thoughts, add images, tag your location, and select a mood. Tap "Save" when you're done. Your entry defaults to private.

photo_camera

Can I add photos and media to entries?

Yes. The rich editor supports inline images from your camera roll or camera. You can also add location tags from your current position or search for a place. Each entry supports multiple photos.

mood

What is mood tracking?

Each journal entry lets you select a mood from a set of options. Over time, DayByDay builds a mood timeline so you can see patterns in how you feel and what influences your wellbeing.

group Social Features

person_add How do I connect with friends?

Go to the Social tab and search for friends by username. Send a connection request and once they accept, you'll see their shared entries in your social feed. You can also share your invite link directly.

share How do I share a journal entry?

Open any entry and tap the share icon. You can share with all your connections, select specific friends, or generate a public link. Entries are always private by default until you explicitly choose to share them.

favorite Can I react to and comment on entries?

Yes. When viewing a friend's shared entry, you can add reactions (emoji-based) and leave text comments. This creates a supportive environment that encourages consistent journaling.

emoji_events Streaks & Gamification

local_fire_department How do streaks work?

Write at least one journal entry per day to build your streak. Your streak count is displayed on your profile and in the leaderboard. Miss a day and your streak resets to zero, so stay consistent.

leaderboard What are leaderboards?

Leaderboards rank you and your connections by current streak length and total entries. It's a friendly way to stay motivated and celebrate each other's consistency.

handshake What are accountability partners?

You can pair up with a friend as accountability partners. You'll both receive reminders to journal and notifications when the other person writes an entry. It helps keep both of you on track.

help Frequently Asked Questions

edit_note How do I create a journal entry?

Tap the "+" button on the home screen to open the rich text editor. Write your thoughts, add images from your camera or photo library, tag your location, and select your mood. Hit "Save" when you're finished. You can edit entries later at any time.

map How does trip organization work?

Go to the Trips tab and tap "New Trip." Give your trip a name, date range, and optional cover photo. Then assign journal entries to the trip. Entries are plotted on an interactive MapKit-powered map so you can relive your journey visually.

local_fire_department How do streaks work?

Write at least one journal entry per day to build your streak. Your current streak and longest streak are displayed on your profile. Daily prompts and notifications help you remember to write before the day ends.

lock How do privacy controls work?

Every entry defaults to private. When sharing, you choose the audience: all connections, select friends, or public link. You can revoke sharing at any time. Your private entries are encrypted and never visible to other users.

person_add How do I connect with friends?

In the Social tab, search by username or share your invite link. Once a friend accepts your connection request, you'll see their shared entries in your feed and can react or comment on them.

download Can I export my journal data?

Yes. Go to Settings and tap "Export Data." You can download all your journal entries as a JSON or PDF file, including images and metadata. Your data belongs to you and you can take it with you at any time.
