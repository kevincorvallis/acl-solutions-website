rocket_launch Getting Started

dynamic_feed

How do I read the daily news feed?

Open the News Daily app from the web, iOS, or PWA. Your personalized feed loads automatically with the latest stories from all aggregated sources. Scroll to browse, or use the search and filter options to find specific topics.

bookmark

How do I create a watchlist?

Navigate to the Watchlists tab and tap "Create Watchlist." Add keywords, topics, companies, or GitHub repos you want to track. You will be notified whenever a new story matches your watchlist criteria.

install_mobile

How do I install the PWA?

Visit News Daily in your mobile browser. On iOS, tap the Share button and then "Add to Home Screen." On Android/Chrome, tap the install banner or the three-dot menu and select "Install app."

podcasts Podcast Help

schedule How often is the podcast released?

The PookieB Weekly podcast is released every week. Each episode is approximately 15 minutes and covers the most significant stories from the past seven days.

record_voice_over Who is the podcast voice?

The podcast uses a custom AI-generated voice powered by ElevenLabs Text-to-Speech technology. It is the PookieB voice, designed to be clear, engaging, and professional.

download Can I download episodes offline?

Yes. When using the PWA or iOS app, you can download podcast episodes for offline listening. Tap the download icon next to any episode.

help Frequently Asked Questions

auto_awesome How does the AI summarization work?

We use OpenAI's GPT-4o-mini model to generate concise summaries of each article. The AI reads the full article content and produces a brief summary with key takeaways, sentiment analysis, and contextual background.

tune Can I customize which sources appear in my feed?

Yes. Go to Settings and select "Sources" to enable or disable individual news outlets, social platforms, and community sources. Your preferences sync across all devices.

lock How is my data kept private?

We collect minimal data -- your reading history and watchlists are stored securely and never shared with third parties for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

phone_iphone Is there an iOS app?

Yes, News Daily is available as a native iOS app and as a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can also access the full experience through any modern web browser.

stream What is real-time streaming (SSE)?

Server-Sent Events (SSE) allow breaking stories to appear in your feed the moment they are published, without needing to refresh the page. If you experience connection issues, try refreshing the page or checking your network connection.
