We're PookieB.

We started building apps because we couldn't find the ones we wanted. A ski trip planner that actually tracked powder. A finance app that spoke plain English. A journaling app worth opening every day. So we built them.

Six products later, we're still a small team in Los Angeles shipping things we use ourselves.

AI that doesn't suck.

We use AI to make things genuinely better, not to slap a chatbot on everything. If AI doesn't improve the experience, we don't ship it.

Design is the product.

If it doesn't feel good to use, it doesn't ship. We sweat the details because that's the difference between software people tolerate and software people love.

Your data, your call.

We collect the minimum. We never sell it. Period. Every product is built with privacy as a constraint, not an afterthought.
