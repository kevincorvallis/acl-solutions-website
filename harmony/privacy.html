Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

1. Introduction

PookieB ("we," "us," or "our") operates Harmony Tracker, an AI-powered relationship coaching and habit tracking application. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our App. By using Harmony Tracker, you agree to the practices described in this policy. Given the deeply personal nature of relationship data, we hold ourselves to the highest standards of data protection.

2. Information We Collect

person Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo. If you sign in via Apple or Google, we receive the information you authorize those providers to share.

favorite Relationship Data

This includes information about your relationship that you voluntarily provide, such as relationship status, partner details, communication preferences, and psychological profile responses. This data is used exclusively to personalize your coaching experience.

tune Tracker Data

We store all tracker entries you create, including streak records, counter values, timer durations, and negative event logs. This data is used to display your progress, generate insights, and share with your paired partner on shared trackers.

smart_toy AI Conversations

Conversations with Nova, including messages you send and AI responses, are stored to provide conversation continuity and context. Messages you paste for analysis are processed by the AI but can be deleted at any time.

link Partner Connections

When you pair with a partner, we store the connection between your accounts, shared tracker permissions, and pairing history. Both partners can see shared tracker data. Unpairing removes shared access but does not delete individual data.

3. Data Sensitivity

shield

We recognize that relationship data is among the most sensitive personal information you can entrust to a service. We treat all Harmony Tracker data with the highest level of care. We will never sell your data, use it for advertising, share it with data brokers, or use it for any purpose beyond operating and improving the App.

4. AI Processing

Harmony Tracker uses GPT-4 by OpenAI to power the Nova AI coach. When you interact with Nova, your messages and relevant context are sent to OpenAI's API for processing.

  • AI conversations are processed via OpenAI's API with data processing agreements in place
  • OpenAI does not use your data to train their models when accessed via the API
  • Conversation context is not stored long-term by OpenAI beyond the processing window
  • We store conversation history locally in our database to provide continuity between sessions
  • You can delete your AI conversation history at any time from the app settings

5. Partner Data Sharing

When you pair with a partner, the following data may be shared between accounts:

  • Tracker data on mutually shared trackers (both partners must opt in)
  • Display name and profile photo
  • Streak and progress milestones on shared trackers

Private trackers, personal AI conversations, message analyses, and conflict documentation are never shared with your partner unless you explicitly choose to share them. You control what is shared at all times.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Harmony Tracker:

Provider Purpose Data Shared
AWS Cloud hosting and storage All app data (encrypted)
Firebase Auth Authentication Email, name, auth tokens
OpenAI (GPT-4) AI coaching (Nova) Conversation messages, context

Each third-party service is governed by its own privacy policy and bound by data processing agreements. We only share the minimum data necessary for each service to function.

7. Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your sensitive relationship data:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3
  • All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256
  • Authentication is handled via Firebase Auth with industry-standard practices
  • Partner pairing codes expire after use and cannot be reused
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Tracker data and AI conversation history can be deleted at any time through the app settings. When you delete your account, we remove all personal data within 30 days, including tracker data, AI conversations, partner connections, and psychological profiles, except where retention is required by law. Data shared with paired partners is removed from shared views upon account deletion.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your tracker data and conversation history
  • Opt out of non-essential data processing
  • Withdraw consent for partner data sharing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below or use the in-app settings.

10. Children's Privacy

Harmony Tracker is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe someone under 18 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or via email. Continued use of Harmony Tracker after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We will never retroactively weaken the privacy protections described in this policy without your explicit consent.
