Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

1. Introduction

PookieB ("we," "us," or "our") operates Shredders, a snow conditions and ski trip planning application. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our iOS app and web application. By using Shredders, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

location_on Location Data

With your permission, we collect your device's location to show nearby resorts, provide relevant road conditions, and enable resort check-ins. Location data is used in real-time and is not stored beyond your active session unless you check in at a resort.

favorite Resort Preferences

We store your favorite resorts, powder alert thresholds, and notification preferences to personalize your experience and deliver relevant alerts.

group Social Data

When you use social features, we collect your check-ins, photos, comments, likes, trip events, RSVP responses, and carpool participation. Your social profile and activity may be visible to other users.

smart_toy AI Chat Data

Conversations with the AI chat feature are sent to our AI providers for processing. Chat history may be stored to provide context for follow-up questions and to improve the service. You can delete your chat history at any time in the app settings.

person Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo. If you sign in via Apple or Google, we receive the information you authorize those providers to share.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and personalize the Shredders experience
  • Send powder alerts and weather notifications
  • Enable social features including check-ins, events, and carpools
  • Process AI chat requests and provide intelligent recommendations
  • Improve our algorithms and powder scoring models
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the App
  • Communicate with you about service updates and support

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Shredders:

Provider Purpose Data Shared
Supabase Authentication, database, storage Account info, preferences, social data
Claude (Anthropic) AI chat and recommendations Chat messages, resort context
GPT-4 (OpenAI) AI analysis and summaries Snow data, user queries

Each third-party service is governed by its own privacy policy. We only share the minimum data necessary for each service to function.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest, secure authentication via Supabase Auth, and regular security reviews. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Check-in history and social content are retained until you delete them. AI chat history can be cleared at any time. When you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt out of non-essential data processing
  • Withdraw consent for location tracking or notifications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below or use the in-app settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Shredders is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or via email. Continued use of Shredders after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
