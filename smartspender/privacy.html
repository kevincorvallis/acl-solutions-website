Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

ACL Solutions LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates SmartSpender. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service. Please read this policy carefully.

database 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Financial Data

  • Transaction records uploaded via CSV or Excel files (dates, amounts, merchant names, descriptions)
  • Budget configurations and spending categories you create
  • Bill splitting records and shared expense data

Receipt Data

  • Receipt images uploaded for scanning
  • Extracted receipt data (merchant name, date, line items, amounts, totals)

Usage Data

  • Natural language queries you submit to the AI
  • Browser type, device information, and IP address
  • Pages visited and features used within the Service

psychology 2. AI Processing

SmartSpender uses third-party AI services to provide its core features:

Anthropic (Claude 3.5 Sonnet)

Used for natural language query processing, transaction categorization, spending analysis, and generating financial insights. Your transaction data and queries are sent to Anthropic’s API for processing.

OpenAI (GPT-4o Vision)

Used for receipt scanning and image parsing. Receipt images are sent to OpenAI’s API for optical character recognition and data extraction.

Both providers process data under their respective data processing agreements. Your data is not used by these providers to train their AI models.

share 3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate SmartSpender:

Amazon Web Services

Cloud infrastructure, hosting, and data storage

Anthropic

AI-powered text analysis and natural language processing

OpenAI

Computer vision for receipt scanning and image parsing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes.

lock 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • check_circle Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL (HTTPS).
  • check_circle Encryption at Rest: Financial data stored on our servers is encrypted using AES-256 encryption.
  • check_circle Secure API Communication: All communication with third-party AI providers uses authenticated, encrypted API connections.
  • check_circle Access Controls: Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel only on a need-to-know basis.

how_to_reg 5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • visibility Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • edit Correction: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • delete Deletion: You may request deletion of your personal data. We will comply unless retention is required by law.
  • download Portability: You may request your data in a portable, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@pookieb.com.

cookie 6. Cookies and Tracking

SmartSpender may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, remember preferences, and analyze usage patterns. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

child_care 7. Children’s Privacy

SmartSpender is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information.

update 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
